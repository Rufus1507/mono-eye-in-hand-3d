# How to take calibration photos

This guide is for anyone sending photos (or a video) of the calibration board so
that their camera can be calibrated remotely. Following it takes about 15
minutes. Every rule below prevents a specific way that calibrations silently go
wrong.

## 1. Print the board

- Print `bang_charuco_A4.pdf` (or `bang_charuco_A3.pdf`) at **100 % / "Actual
  size"**. Turn off "Fit to page".
- Use **matte** paper if you can. Glossy paper reflects light and hides corners.
- Glue or tape it **completely flat** onto something rigid: foam board, thick
  cardboard, a clipboard, a flat tabletop. A bent board is the most common cause
  of a wrong result.

You do **not** need to measure the squares. The field of view does not depend on
the printed size.

## 2. Set up the camera

- Use **the exact mode you will use later**: the same resolution, the same lens,
  and the same video/photo mode. A calibration belongs to one camera **and** one
  mode.
- **Do not zoom.** On phones, zoom often switches to a different lens.
- **Lock the focus** if the camera allows it. On most phones: tap and hold on the
  board until "AE/AF lock" appears. If focus cannot be locked, keep the board at
  roughly the same distance in every photo.
- Turn off "beauty", "AI scene", HDR and wide-angle distortion correction if the
  camera offers these options.
- Even light, no direct reflections on the board.

## 3. Take 30 to 60 photos

Keep the camera still and move the board, or the other way round. Either works.

- **Cover the whole frame, especially the four corners and the edges.** Corners
  are where lens distortion is strongest, and where it has to be measured.
- **Tilt the board 20 to 45 degrees** in different directions: left, right, up,
  down and diagonally. This is the most important rule. Without tilt, focal
  length and distortion cannot be separated.
- Let the board fill a **large part** of the image in most photos.
- It is fine if part of the board is outside the image.
- **Hold still** for each photo. Motion blur makes a photo unusable.
- Change the position or angle between photos. Near-identical photos are
  removed automatically and do not help.

## 4. Send the original files

- **Do not** edit, crop, resize, rotate or filter the photos.
- Send the **original files**. Many chat apps compress photos, so send them as
  files or documents, or as a zip archive.
- Only send photos from one camera and one mode together. For a second camera,
  send a separate folder.

## Alternative: a video

Instead of photos, you can record a video of 1 to 2 minutes in the mode you want
calibrated. Move the board slowly through the positions above and **pause about
one second** at each position. The pauses are where sharp frames are taken from.

## What you get back

For every photo, you learn whether it was used and, if not, why (blurry, board
not found, duplicate pose, different resolution or lens). If too few photos are
usable, you get a short list of what to reshoot instead of a result that cannot
be trusted.
